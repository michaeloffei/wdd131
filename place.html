About Ghana

Ghana is a West African country known for its rich cultural heritage, gold mining, cocoa production, and friendly people. Accra is the capital city and home to major landmarks such as Independence Square.

Quick Facts

  • Independence Day: 6 March
  • Major Exports: Cocoa, Gold, Oil
  • National Flower: Gold Coast lily
